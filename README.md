# Khata Book (ખાતાવહી)

Income, expense and credit (udhar) ledger in Gujarati and English by Darshankumar Savsani (DARSHAN CORPORATION).

**Website (works on phone and PC, no install):** https://1darshankumarsavsani-blip.github.io/Khata-Book/

## Latest: V3.0 (Android + website)

- **Appearance** in Settings: 8 theme colours, dark mode, 4 text sizes, bold text, high contrast.
- **Monthly budgets** per category (Report tab) with progress bars and a warning on the Ledger when a category passes 80%.
- **Search and filters** on the ledger (note or amount, income/expense, category).
- **Excel/CSV export** of all entries and credit transactions.
- Still included from V2: Google sync and backup, PIN lock, statement PDF per person, credit due dates with a 9 AM reminder, category chart.
- Download `Khata_Book_V3.apk` (Android, installs over older versions and keeps data) or `Khata_Book_Windows.exe` (opens the website in its own window; Edge or Chrome needed) from the latest release.

Honest note: tested on a simulated Android 14 phone and a headless browser only. Sign-in, PDF and CSV sharing and reminders need a real phone to confirm. Older versions are on the Releases page.

Source code: `app/` (Java, Android) and `docs/` (website).
